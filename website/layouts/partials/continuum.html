Air-gapped

Same Process, Just a Media Break

Other air-gap tools make you build a second pipeline, with separate packaging, separate inventory and separate trust. With Harbor Satellite, an air-gapped site is the same satellite in the same fleet. Ground Control produces a signed, differential OCI bundle in place of a live sync. You carry it across, the satellite verifies and imports it, and the site's state stays consistent with the fleet. When the site reconnects, replication picks up from the last applied bundle.

Harbor Satellite is the only registry solution that keeps one process across the whole spectrum. The air gap is a transport detail, not a second workflow.
