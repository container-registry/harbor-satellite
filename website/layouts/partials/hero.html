Harbor Satellite 1.0 · Open source, part of the CNCF Harbor project

Your Harbor Registry at Every Edge Site

Harbor Satellite keeps a local copy of exactly the artifacts each site is assigned, serves them to the site's container runtime, and keeps serving when the link to the cloud is down.

Assign artifacts once in Ground Control. Every satellite in the fleet converges, and you can see which site holds what.
